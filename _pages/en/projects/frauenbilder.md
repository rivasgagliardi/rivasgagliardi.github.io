**University of Cologne, since 2024**

My Habilitation project examines how women writers of the early modern Romance world — Marguerite de Navarre (L’Heptaméron, 1559) and María de Zayas (Novelas amorosas y ejemplares, 1637; Desengaños amorosos, 1647) — shaped the frame narrative into a space of female speech and judgement. At its centre lies the question of who may take the floor in the staged conversation — in the cadre, in the sarao —, before whom, and with what claim to knowledge: storytelling emerges here as a form of critical knowledge about love, marriage, violence and the social order. The project brings together narratology, gender history and the tradition of the querelle des femmes — and asks, finally, what becomes of these spaces of women’s speech, the salon, the tertulia, when the nineteenth-century novel occupies them anew.
